HOW MISSIONS WORK

Set GPS, walk into the beam, press E

Press J on desktop (or tap Jobs on mobile) to open the Missions journal. On desktop, press keys 1 through 0, -, or = to pin GPS directly to a mission. Travel to the glowing beam at the location and press E to start. Need a reset? Press X twice on desktop to abandon an active mission, or use Restart mission in the Esc pause menu.

Best starter order & unlocks

Start with 1-dot missions

If you are learning the streets and controls, begin with the three 1/3 difficulty jobs: Bin Chicken Wrangler (Queen Street Mall), Riverfire (South Bank) and Jacaranda Snaps (QUT Gardens Point). They sit close to central landmarks and help you build early cash.

Unlock the Lyme helmet

The Missions journal explicitly notes: Helmet: locked (win Swooping Season). Head to Postie Pete in City Botanic Gardens (GPS key 4) and beat Swooping Season to unlock the helmet, which you can then toggle with H while riding a Lyme.

Highest payout jobs

Route 199 ($60–140) starts right beside City Hall at Adelaide St Stop 19, while E-Bike Crew ($40–150) at Post Office Square and Bus Grand Prix ($20–150) at North Quay offer the top ceiling payouts once you are comfortable driving and riding.

Pair with the map & controls

Use M to see every mission marker across the Brisbane River before setting off, and keep our controls reference handy for bus doors (B), wheelies (Space) and mission abandon (X ×2).
