Behind the wheel?

WASD steers and drives. Shift or Space acts as a handbrake. E gets you in or out.

Two thumbs. A whole city.

↔

Left thumb

Move, drive or ride. Push to the edge to sprint.

◎

Right thumb

Look around. Use two fingers to adjust the camera.

E

On-screen buttons

Use the labelled buttons for interaction, the map and jobs. Jump becomes a brake while driving.

Weapons & riding

Open Weapons to select equipment. Hold and release Throw / Swing to use it. Riding controls include a bell and Wheelie; the Metro has Hover.

More actions & online play

Open ••• for outfit, night, bus doors and other actions. Use II for settings. Enable Voice before holding the microphone button; the online player list offers mute and report.
