IN-GAME NAVIGATION

How to read the Brisbane Streets map

Press M on desktop (or tap Map on mobile) to open the full city overlay, and press M again to close it. Coloured circular pins mark active mission givers across both sides of the Brisbane River, while the top-right corner of your HUD keeps a live circular minimap visible as you walk, ride or drive.

Key districts & landmarks on the map

King George Square & City Hall

Your starting plaza right outside Brisbane City Hall. If you ever get lost or stuck, press Esc and select Return to City Hall to teleport back to this central hub. Route 199 also begins right next door at Adelaide St Stop 19.

Queen Street Mall & Central CBD

The busy heart of the city just southeast of King George Square (your opening objective points straight here). Key mission stops clustered across the CBD include Bin Chicken Wrangler, Pie Floater Express, Busker’s Bucket and E-Bike Crew at Post Office Square.

Roma Street Parkland & Wickham Park

Head northwest from the CBD toward Roma Street station and the green hillside trails of Roma St Parkland and Wickham Park. You will find Speccy Season in the parkland and Brisbane Storm Chaser near the Old Windmill.

City Botanic Gardens & QUT Gardens Point

Occupying the southeastern river bend of the CBD peninsula, this leafy district hosts Maroon Pride, Swooping Season (the helmet unlock job) and Jacaranda Snaps near QUT Gardens Point, linking across the river via Goodwill Bridge.

South Bank & River Crossings

Cross Victoria Bridge, Kurilpa Bridge or Goodwill Bridge over the Brisbane River to reach South Bank. The map marks GOMA, State Library, the Brisbane Sign, the Wheel of Brisbane, Streets Beach and the Riverfire mission marker.

Kangaroo Point & Fortitude Valley

Kangaroo Point sits across the eastern bend of the river opposite City Botanic Gardens. On the northeastern edge of the map, the developer has marked Fortitude Valley — Coming Soon for future expansion.

STREET-LEVEL ORIENTATION

Minimap + objective distance tracker

Once you close the full map, the top-left Objective box shows your current destination and distance in metres (such as Head down to the Queen Street Mall · 176 m SE), while the top-right minimap tracks nearby streets, parks and mission pins in real time.
