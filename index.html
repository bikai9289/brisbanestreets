Missions in Brisbane Streets Game:

Keep the controls handy ↗

Open your missions

Press J on desktop to open missions. On touch, tap Jobs. Read the active objective before heading across the city so you know where to travel next.

Check the route

Open the in-game city map with M, or tap Map on mobile. Pair the map with your mission tracker to plan turns, bridges and shortcuts.

Need a fresh attempt?

The pause menu includes Restart mission while a job is active. On desktop, pressing X twice abandons the current mission so you can pick another.

Mission-menu instructions are based on the in-game interface rather than a completion walkthrough or reward table.

VEHICLES & CITY LIFE / GETTING AROUND

Vehicles, Riding & City Life in Brisbane Streets Game.

Full vehicle keybinds ↗

Driving cars, buses & QPS vehicles

Press E to enter or exit nearby vehicles, steer with W A S D, and use Shift or Space as a handbrake. While driving you can honk with H, signal with Z and X, look behind with Q, and tune the radio using 1–5 or R. On a bus, B operates the doors; in a QPS car, Y toggles lights and siren.

Riding Lyme scooters & bikes

Two-wheeled rides use familiar movement keys with a few extra touches. Hold Space while riding to pull a wheelie and release before tipping back, press Q to ring the bell, and press H to toggle your helmet on a Lyme. When driving the Metro, F or left click toggles hover and land.

On-foot actions & Brisbane flavour

On foot you can sprint with Shift, jump with Space, talk to characters with T (or hold U in Chrome and Edge for speech input), and switch outfits with C or skip to night with N. Local touches include pressing I to shoo a bin chicken away from your food, G to give $2, and P to call 000 in the game.

Weapons, equipment & combat

Hold Tab to open the weapon wheel, select an item with the mouse or arrow keys, and release to equip it. Press E to pick up nearby gear, hold and release 1 or left click to swing or throw, and press 2 to drop what you are holding. Unarmed keys cover punch (F), block (Q), dodge (Ctrl) and kick or shove (Z / R).

Because keys overlap between walking, driving and riding, check the Brisbane Streets controls reference whenever you switch transport.

ONLINE & DEVICES / DESKTOP TO MOBILE

Desktop vs. mobile play
& online chat basics.

Brisbane Streets runs in modern WebGL 2 browsers on both computers and phones, with layout differences worth knowing before you join an online room.

Performance & WebGL help ↗
PC

Keyboard, mouse & voice keys

Use Enter to open online text chat and Tab to switch between room and nearby chat. Enable voice in settings first, then hold V for nearby voice chat.

MOB

Two-thumb touch controls

Move or steer with the left thumb, pan the camera with the right thumb, and open the ••• menu for outfits, night toggle, bus doors and extra actions.

FPS

Graphics & browser performance

If frame rates drop on laptops or phones, lower Quality in Graphics settings, turn off ambient occlusion under Advanced, and click Save & reload.

03 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things before you go.

Is this the official Brisbane Streets website?+

No. This is an independent fan guide. The Play buttons link straight to brisbane-streets.pages.dev, where the game is hosted.

Can I play on my phone?+

The official game includes touch controls. Performance varies by device and browser. See our mobile controls.

Why is the game not loading?+

Check the official URL, try an updated browser, and review our loading checklist. Network restrictions may still prevent access.

How do I move my save to another device?+

Export your save from the game’s Settings, then import the file in the destination browser. Follow the save backup steps before clearing site data.

How do I drive cars, buses or ride a Lyme scooter in Brisbane Streets?+

Walk up to a vehicle and press E (or the on-screen interact button on mobile) to enter. Use WASD to drive, B for bus doors, Y for QPS sirens, and Space to pull a wheelie while riding. See the full controls guide for every vehicle key.

How do online chat and voice chat work?+

On desktop, press Enter to open text chat, Tab to switch between room and nearby chat, and hold V for nearby voice chat after turning voice on in settings. On mobile, enable Voice before holding the microphone button, and use the player list to mute or report players.

Where is the metro code guide?+

We haven’t verified a metro code walkthrough yet. We won’t publish guessed codes or confuse them with the online test code field.
